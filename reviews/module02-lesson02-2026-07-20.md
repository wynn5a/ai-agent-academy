## Findings (most severe first)

### [Polish] `max_completion_tokens` justification is narrower than the real reason
- **Where:** `content/modules/module02/lessons/02-react-and-planning.ts:204` (OpenAI ReAct variant explanation)
- **Standard:** 1 (Factual accuracy) / 2 (Clarity)
- **Issue:** The explanation attributes `max_completion_tokens` specifically to "reasoning models rejected the old `max_tokens`." In current Chat Completions, `max_tokens` is deprecated *generally* in favor of `max_completion_tokens` (reasoning models hard-reject it; non-reasoning models accept it but it's the deprecated spelling). Pinning the reason to "reasoning models" is the sharper-sounding but slightly-off half of the truth.
- **Why it matters:** A learner who repeats "you only need `max_completion_tokens` on reasoning models" in an interview states the deprecation too narrowly — a precise interviewer would correct it. M1 L2 already teaches this area with per-version discipline; this line is a notch looser than the course's own bar.
- **Suggested fix:** Soften to "…and note `max_completion_tokens`, which superseded the now-deprecated `max_tokens` on Chat Completions (reasoning models reject the old name outright)." Keeps the interview-relevant fact, corrects the scope.

### [Polish] Legacy-block framing vs. reasoning-era param sits in mild tension
- **Where:** `content/modules/module02/lessons/02-react-and-planning.ts:175-204` (OpenAI ReAct variant: `stop=[...]` + `max_completion_tokens` on Chat Completions, framed as "the legacy shape on purpose")
- **Standard:** 2 (Clarity)
- **Issue:** The block deliberately uses Chat Completions + `stop` as the pre-tool-calling text protocol, yet also uses `max_completion_tokens` and cites reasoning-model behavior. A sharp learner may wonder: if this is a reasoning-capable model (why else the `max_completion_tokens` note?), does `stop` even apply, and why not the Responses API? The explanation gestures at this ("on a modern reasoning model you'd reach for the Responses API") but never says the quiet part: this text-protocol demo assumes reasoning dialed down/off, which is exactly why `stop` + reading `message.content` still works cleanly.
- **Why it matters:** Minor, but the lesson's whole register rewards learners who notice these seams. One clause resolving it would preempt the doubt and model the per-version thinking the course prizes.
- **Suggested fix:** Add a half-sentence to the variant explanation, e.g. "(this legacy shape assumes reasoning is minimal/off — which is why `stop` and plain `message.content` behave predictably here)."
